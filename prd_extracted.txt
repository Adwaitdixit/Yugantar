SIH positioningDharohar Setu is not another heritage-information portal. It is a participatory cultural-preservation layer: communities and field collectors can capture local knowledge, the system helps organize it, provenance is retained, and visitors can experience it through an immersive cultural atlas.
1. Product Vision
India's living culture exists not only in monuments and official records, but also in voices, songs, recipes, crafts, rituals, oral histories, local languages, and community practices. Dharohar Setu aims to make this knowledge easier to preserve and discover without stripping it of its original context or ownership.
The platform follows one core principle:
Tell the story once. Preserve its provenance. Let the community control where it travels.A single Cultural Record can power discovery, audio storytelling, multilingual access, community review, and future learning/institutional integrations—without repeatedly asking a knowledge-holder to submit the same story.
1.1 Product principles
Community-first: communities are contributors and knowledge holders, not merely content sources.
Offline-first: collection must remain possible in low-connectivity environments.
Original-first: original audio/video and original-language content remain authoritative records.
Provenance-first: every record retains who/where/when/how/source information where consent permits.
AI-assisted, not AI-authoritative: AI organizes and assists; it does not declare cultural truth.
Consent-aware: public, community-only, and restricted knowledge are treated differently at the data/query layer.
Museum-like discovery: the public experience should feel like exploring a digital cultural museum, not a database.

5. Core User Journey
A field collector or contributor selects a cultural record type: Oral Story, Folk Song, Recipe, Craft, Tradition, or Heritage Place.
The contributor records audio/video or enters available information. The capture flow works offline.
The app stores the record locally and marks it as pending synchronization.
When connectivity returns, the record synchronizes to the server.
AI assists with language detection, transcription, translation, summarization, categorization, keywords, and—where relevant—claim extraction.
The system attaches provenance: contributor/collector, location, date, original media, source, consent tier and relevant evidence.
Community or authorized reviewers can correct, approve, reject, or flag the record.
Public users can discover verified/public records through the immersive map and cultural story pages.
Hero demoAn elder's local story is recorded offline → synchronized later → transcribed and translated → provenance is shown → community verification is recorded → the story appears on the map → a visitor listens to the original voice.

14. Consent, Ethics & Data Governance
Public: discoverable by public users, subject to the contributor's consent.
Community-only: visible only to authorized community/reviewer audiences.
Sacred-restricted: never exposed outside the explicitly consented audience; access control must be enforced at the query/data layer.
Documentation consent and future commercial use are separate permissions.
Original-language audio is preserved as the authoritative record for multilingual/endangered-language content.
AI-generated translations/summaries must not overwrite original material.
Contributors should be able to correct or request review of records where appropriate.
Sensitive location information should not be exposed if doing so could harm a community, site, or practice.

19. Key Judge Questions — Prepared Answers
Q: How are you different from ASI/government portals?
A: We are not replacing them. We add a community/field-collection and living-culture layer for oral stories, songs, recipes and local practices, while allowing institutional evidence to be referenced where available.
Q: How can AI verify history?
A: AI does not decide truth. It extracts claims and retrieves potential evidence. A human/community reviewer makes the final verification decision, and the evidence remains visible.
Q: What if there is no historical evidence?
A: We preserve the account as oral tradition or community knowledge rather than falsely labeling it as documented history.
Q: How will remote communities use it without internet?
A: Collectors can record offline. Records remain in a local queue and synchronize when connectivity returns.
Q: Why do you need AI?
A: Many contributions are audio and multilingual. AI reduces transcription, translation, classification and evidence-search effort while preserving the original recording.
Q: Why not build all five modules?
A: The modules are future applications of the same Core. For the internal round, we prove the Core through one complete journey and show the other modules as roadmap extensions.
20. Final Product Positioning
The pitchDharohar Setu is a living cultural atlas—not a static heritage directory. It helps communities preserve local knowledge in their own voices, works in low-connectivity environments, uses AI to make that knowledge searchable and multilingual, keeps provenance visible, and lets people explore India's cultural diversity through an immersive digital experience.
Preserve locally. Verify responsibly. Explore globally.
This edited PRD is intentionally MVP-first for SIH internal evaluation. Production claims, national-scale coverage, full institutional integrations, commerce, and advanced knowledge-graph infrastructure remain future work.
